# Andres* – "Great professionals... they did it in the agreed time and budget. We will count on you for the next projects."

## Profile
Andres*, 41*, manages several properties* in Valencia. Follows many files at once with Voltia. Came through a recommendation*.

## Background
His first project was hard because he had very little time, and Voltia delivered on time and budget. He now sends Voltia every new project in Valencia. Some files take two weeks, so most of his files close fast (P4). He knows the retailer from the distributor*.

## Goals
- Keep many files moving at once.
- Close each one on the date he planned*.
- Keep working with the same people.

## Pains and frustrations
- Having to sign papers on time, which he sometimes forgets (P3).
- A rare file that stays open longer than the others (P4).
- Phoning for news when a file goes quiet (P1).
- Not knowing whom to blame if a file stops (P2).

## Jobs to be done
- **Functional:** When I have many files, I want to see which ones need me, so none stops because of me.
- **Personal:** I want to feel in control of my projects.
- **Social:** I want my clients* to see that I deliver, and that I chose the right installer.

## A day in their life during the wait
He does not wait for one file; he works on all of them. He signs the papers when asked*, answers Voltia quickly*, and calls only when a file is quiet. He talks with Marc*, the person he knows, and not with a call centre.

## How they look for news today
He asks Voltia first, by message*. They tell him honestly that a file may take long. He rarely calls the retailer or the distributor.

## What makes them trust or doubt an installer
- Trust: a named person, work done on time and budget, and an honest warning about delays (P5).
- Doubt: a file that goes quiet with no explanation (P1).
- Complaints are not followed up at Voltia, so he would not know where to raise one (P5).
