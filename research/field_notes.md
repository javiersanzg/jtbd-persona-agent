# Field Research - Team 3 Voltia

*The names of the company, its staff and its client are changed in this public copy (also inside the quotes). Everything else is real and quoted word for word.*

**Challenge:** Voltia clients with an Iberdrola (i-DE) file or an electrical certificate in progress do not know what is happening with it.

Quotes are word for word, in Spanish, with our translation in brackets.

## 1. Interview at Voltia

### Toni, admin and sales

Recorded WhatsApp interview, 05/10/2026 (three voice notes and written follow-up answers). Toni answers the clients who call about their files. He is also a member of our team. He reviewed this transcript on 23/11/2026 and corrected two answers, marked below.

**What do clients ask about most these days?**
«Están constantemente comunicándose con nosotros porque son procesos que se alargan mucho» (They are constantly contacting us because these processes take a long time)

**What happens with Iberdrola files and certificates?**
«Se atascan mucho, es un jaleo constante, hay que subir mucha documentación» (They get stuck a lot, it is a constant mess, you have to upload a lot of documents)
«A veces el cliente se olvida de que tiene que firmar unos papeles y tienes que ir detrás de él, a veces a los compañeros se les olvida hacer una fotografía y [...] tienen que volver al sitio» (Sometimes the client forgets to sign some papers and you have to chase him, sometimes the team forgets to take a photo and has to go back to the site)

**How often do you go back to a site for a photo or a paper?**
«2 o 3» (Two or three times), per month. Corrected by Toni on 23/11/2026.

**How long does a file take?**
«Hay expedientes que duran dos años o expedientes que duran dos semanas. Depende de lo que haya que hacer» (Some files take two years and some take two weeks. It depends on what has to be done)

**What do you tell a client who calls about Iberdrola?**
«Se le comunica que es posible que el expediente se tarde. Y se intenta ser muy transparente en ese sentido» (We tell him the file may take long, and we try to be very transparent about it)

**How do clients find you?**
«Generalmente por contactos y recomendaciones» (Mostly through contacts and recommendations)

**How is the work shared out?**
«Marc es quien más tiene trato con el cliente» (Marc is the one who deals most with the client)

**When a job goes badly or very well, do you talk about it afterwards?**
«Se hace una reunión» (We hold a meeting). Corrected by Toni on 23/11/2026.

## 3. Context from outside Voltia

These are not interviews and not Voltia clients. They are public forum posts by people waiting for the same kind of file, most of them with i-DE, and two published expert interviews. We used them to see the extreme cases that Voltia's clients can face.

### People waiting for their file (public forum posts)

**Julio21**, new supply with i-DE, 04/06/2025, https://www.foroelectricidad.net/threads/acometida-i-de-iberdrola.5744/
«Yo llamo por teléfono día sí y día no para preguntar siempre me dicen lo mismo y no me dan solución alguna» (I phone every other day to ask, they always tell me the same thing and give me no solution)
«Está todo perfectamente realizado y no puedo tener luz por este trámite» (Everything is done perfectly and I cannot have electricity because of this paperwork)

**PUMA**, power increase, 16/03/2024, https://nergiza.com/foro/threads/aumento-de-potencia-con-iberdrola.13320/
«Mi comercializadora Iberdrola, se desentiende de este tema y echa la culpa a Eléctrica Conquense» (My retailer Iberdrola washes its hands of it and blames Eléctrica Conquense)
«Llevo así desde el día 3 de octubre de 2023, pasando frío y con la única luz de unas bombillas, no funciona nada» (I have been like this since 3 October 2023, cold and with only a few light bulbs, nothing works)
«Puedo denunciarlos por estos motivos, o solo me queda ajo y agua» (Can I report them for this, or do I just have to put up with it?)

**pantaleon**, new supply with i-DE, 19/09/2024, https://foro.clubtesla.es/index.php?topic=11139.0
«La comercializadora me dice que la distribuidora rechaza la solicitud y la distribuidora que la comercializadora» (The retailer says the distributor rejects the request, and the distributor says the retailer does)
«Este trabajo me está llevando 5 horas diarias y sigue embuclado» (This is taking me 5 hours a day and it is still stuck in a loop)

**Tevi62**, solar self-consumption file with Iberdrola, 07/10/2023 to 10/10/2023, https://nergiza.com/foro/threads/autorizacion-autoconsumo-iberdrola.12384/
«[...] no hacen más que decirme el documento que les mando no se ve la URL y me tienen loco» (They keep telling me the URL in the document I send cannot be seen, and they are driving me mad)
«Esta vez, que es la cuarta que los mandan, vuelven a estar rechazados» (This time, the fourth time they send them, they are rejected again)
«impotencia» (helplessness)

### Experts (published interviews)

**The CEO of i-DE**, Energías Renovables, 26/09/2024, https://www.energias-renovables.com/entrevistas/las-redes-son-el-gigante-silencioso-20240926
On solar self-consumption files: «En 2023 i-DE activó el 59% de los expedientes en menos de cinco días; y el 93% en menos de 20 días» (In 2023 i-DE activated 59% of the files in under five days and 93% in under 20 days)

**Joan Herrera**, director of Environmental Action and Energy, El Prat city council, Energías Renovables, 21/06/2023, https://www.energias-renovables.com/autoconsumo/las-grandes-distribuidoras-bloquean-el-autoconsumo-20230612
«Muchas veces los propios errores de las distribuidoras lo que hacen es empantanar la tramitación» (Many times the distributors' own errors bog down the process)
«En nuestro caso tenemos 15 instalaciones que llevan tramitándose 15 meses» (In our case we have 15 installations that have been in process for 15 months)
