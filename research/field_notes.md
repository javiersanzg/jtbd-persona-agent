# Field Research - Team 3 Voltia

*The names of the company, its staff and its client are changed in this public copy (also inside the quotes). Everything else is real and quoted word for word.*

**Challenge:** Voltia clients with an Iberdrola (i-DE) file or an electrical certificate in progress do not know what is happening with it.

All quotes are copied word for word from their source, in Spanish, with our English translation in brackets. The online quotes were checked against the live pages on 23/11/2026. In the left column we only write what can be checked: the question we asked, or the facts of the post. We do not write our own interpretation there.

## 1. Toni, admin and sales at Voltia

**WHO:** Toni, admin and sales at Voltia for a year and a half, and member of our team · **WHERE:** three WhatsApp voice notes (about 13 minutes) and written answers to follow-up questions · **WHEN:** 05/10/2026

| QUESTION WE ASKED | WHAT HE SAID |
|---|---|
| ¿Qué es lo que más preguntan los clientes últimamente? | «Están constantemente comunicándose con nosotros porque son procesos que se alargan mucho» (They are constantly contacting us because these processes take a long time) |
| ¿Qué pasa con los trámites con Iberdrola o los certificados? | «Se atascan mucho, es un jaleo constante, hay que subir mucha documentación» (They get stuck a lot, it is a constant mess, you have to upload a lot of documents) and «a veces el cliente se olvida de que tiene que firmar unos papeles y tienes que ir detrás de él, a veces a los compañeros se les olvida hacer una fotografía y [...] tienen que volver al sitio» (sometimes the client forgets to sign some papers and you have to chase him, sometimes the team forgets to take a photo and has to go back to the site) |
| Written follow-up: how often do you go back to a site for a photo or a paper? | «Cuatro o cinco veces» (Four or five times), per month |
| Written follow-up: how long does an Iberdrola file take? | «Hay expedientes que duran dos años o expedientes que duran dos semanas. Depende de lo que haya que hacer» (Some files take two years and some take two weeks. It depends on what has to be done) |
| Written follow-up: what do you tell a client who calls about Iberdrola? | «Se le comunica que es posible que el expediente se tarde. Y se intenta ser muy transparente en ese sentido» (We tell him the file may take long, and we try to be very transparent about it) |
| ¿Cómo llegan los clientes? | «Generalmente por contactos y recomendaciones» (Mostly through contacts and recommendations) |
| ¿Cómo se reparten los trabajos? | «Marc es quien más tiene trato con el cliente» (Marc is the one who deals most with the client) |
| Si en obra notas una queja que se repite, ¿cómo llega a la oficina? | «No se suele hacer un seguimiento de las quejas» (Complaints are not usually followed up) |
| Cuando un trabajo sale mal o muy bien, ¿se habla después? | «En los casos en los que hay algún error que se repite, pues no llevamos un control estricto para evitar que vuelva a suceder» (When a mistake repeats, we do not keep a strict control to stop it happening again) |
