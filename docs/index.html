How it works

  1. Goal

    Three personas, one per point of the research line, each passing the checker, plus a report that compares them. Only the research as input: no web, no invented data, no solutions.

  2. Brain

    Claude, running headless in Claude Code. It chooses its next step on its own.

  3. Tools

    Read the research, write files, and run check_persona.py: sections in order, all five patterns used, every section traced to a pattern, invented details flagged, no solutions, one page.

  4. Memory

    A step log it writes as it works. Think, act, observe, reflect: every decision is on the record below.

Hard end · A file that is very slow or blocked

Rafael, the restaurant owner

“Every week I asked: is there news? And every week: we are waiting for Iberdrola.”

PASS454 wordschecked by check_persona.py

Profile

  • 58 years old, owner of a small restaurant in Benimaclet, 12 tables.
  • Client of Voltia with a file of 19 months, still open. Point of the line: hard end.
  • Keeps a plastic folder behind the bar with every paper.

Background

Needed more power for a new kitchen. The file started in April of last year. He still cooks on the old cooker. He called Iberdrola once: forty minutes on hold, then they only talk to the installer. Technicians came three times for photos.

Goals

  • Open the new kitchen and stop postponing it.
  • Know where the file is, even if the answer is "still waiting".
  • Have the installer get things right the first time.

Pains and frustrations

  • Weekly answer is always the same: waiting for Iberdrola.
  • Three visits for forgotten photos: "if they forget the photos, what else do they forget?"
  • Had to tell his cook the kitchen would not be ready for summer; she had planned the menu.
  • Distrusts e-mail: "they get lost".

Jobs to be done

  • Functional: know what is missing and who has the file now.
  • Personal: feel that someone is really looking at his file.
  • Social: give his cook and staff a believable date, so he does not lose face.*

A day in their life during the wait

Lunch service ends, and at 16:30 he has a short break. He checks the old cooker, then WhatsApp.* On Thursday, his chosen day, he calls Voltia: the sticky note on the till says "Voltia - jueves". He asks Marc for news. Marc answers, but knows no more than he does. In the evening he adds another dated note to the folder.*

How they look for news today

  • Calls Marc on Thursdays, not every day.
  • Scrolls WhatsApp up to April of last year to find the first message.
  • Checks the paper folder, not e-mail.
  • Called Iberdrola once; never again.

What makes them trust or doubt an installer

  • Trust: a person who always answers (Marc). Rafael still says "he is a good guy".
  • Doubt: repeated visits for photos, and the same answer every week.
  • Doubt grows from silence, not from the length of the wait.

Based on

  • Profile and Background: P4 (forgotten photos and papers shape the whole story)
  • Goals: P1 (he wants to know someone is looking)
  • Pains: P4 and P3 (the answer "waiting for Iberdrola" says nothing)
  • Jobs to be done: P1 and P2 (Marc is the person he counts on)
  • A day in their life: P5 (no small proof of progress arrives*)
  • Looking for news: P2 and P3 (a person, not official messages)
  • Trust or doubt: P4, P2 and P1

Easy end · Follows many files with ease

Laura, the coordinator

“If it is not in the sheet, it does not exist.”

PASS436 wordschecked by check_persona.py

Profile

  • 34 years old, project coordinator at the solar panel company that works with Voltia, in Paterna.
  • Follows about 40 files at the same time. Point of the line: easy end.
  • Works at two screens: a shared spreadsheet and WhatsApp Web.

Background

Her team had a bad year with delayed files. After it, she built a checklist of documents and photos per type of job, printed and stuck on the wall. Now the technician cannot leave the site without the photos.

Goals

  • Know in ten seconds why any file is red.
  • Keep every client calm, even when nothing has changed.
  • Catch missing papers on day one, not later.

Pains and frustrations

  • "Ninety per cent of the delays are a paper that nobody asked for on day one."
  • A status like "waiting for Iberdrola" tells her nothing; she wants a name and a need.
  • Network works and city permits: nobody controls them.*

Jobs to be done

  • Functional: know who has the ball and what they need.
  • Personal: feel in control of 40 files.
  • Social: be the person her own clients count on, with a message every two weeks.

A day in their life during the wait

She starts at her desk and scans the coloured rows: green, amber, red.* She opens one red row and sees the missing item, for example a signed authorisation. Her phone buzzes three times; one answer goes as a voice note in 15 seconds. Every two weeks each client gets a message from her, even with no news.

How they look for news today

  • The shared sheet: one row per client, a "next step" column and a "who" column with a person's name, never a company.
  • WhatsApp for quick contact.
  • She does not rely on the distributor's own status.

What makes them trust or doubt an installer

  • Trust: the installer asks for everything at the start, and the photos are complete before leaving the site.
  • Doubt: documents asked for late, which add weeks.
  • She knows clients want to feel that someone is looking at their file: "They don't want the file faster. Well, yes."

Based on

  • Profile and Background: P4 (her checklist was born from missing papers)
  • Goals: P1 and P5 (regular news, even without news, keeps clients calm)
  • Pains: P3 and P4 (empty status, late papers)
  • Jobs to be done: P3 and P2 (a name for every next step)
  • A day in their life: P5 and P1
  • Looking for news: P3 (a named owner and a next step)
  • Trust or doubt: P4 and P1

Regular · A normal file

Pilar, the nurse

“I don't call the company. I call Marc.”

PASS401 wordschecked by check_persona.py

Profile

  • 42 years old, nurse, homeowner in Torrent.
  • Client of Voltia for a power increase for a heat pump. File of about two months, now finished. Point of the line: regular.
  • Works shifts; her fridge has a calendar full of them.

Background

She asked for more power for the heat pump. The file went normally. The distributor sent e-mails with a number and a code. She does not know what they mean, so she does not open them.

Goals

  • Get the heat pump working without disturbing her shifts.
  • Know whether the file will be fast or slow, so she can stop worrying.*
  • Sign what she must sign at a time she can answer.

Pains and frustrations

  • "They call me when I am at work, to sign something."
  • Unopened e-mails with codes she cannot read.
  • "In the end it was fast. But I didn't know it was going to be fast, so I worried for nothing."

Jobs to be done

  • Functional: know what happens next and when.
  • Personal: stop worrying about a file that is normal.
  • Social: have a person she can count on, not a company.

A day in their life during the wait

She wakes for an early shift* and works at the hospital.* Her phone rings at work and she cannot answer. Later she looks at the heat pump display and the unopened e-mails.* She calls Marc, saved in her phone as "Electricista Marc".

How they look for news today

  • She calls Marc, never the company.
  • She leaves the distributor e-mails unread.
  • She waits for news to reach her; she does not go looking.*

What makes them trust or doubt an installer

  • Trust: one known person who picks up.
  • Doubt: calls at the wrong time, and silence that makes a fast file feel risky.
  • Official codes and statuses do not build trust for her.

Based on

  • Profile and Background: P3 (e-mails with a code mean nothing)
  • Goals: P1 (knowing the file is normal)
  • Pains: P3 and P1 (she worried for nothing); P4 (a signature is asked for late, in the middle of her shift)
  • Jobs to be done: P3 and P2 (a person, not a company)
  • A day in their life: P5 (no small sign of progress reaches her*)
  • Looking for news: P2 and P3
  • Trust or doubt: P2 and P1

What the agent concluded

Challenge: clients with an Iberdrola file or an electrical certificate in progress do not know what is happening with it. Patterns P1 to P5 are in output/patterns.md.

What the three share (common jobs to be done)

  • Functional: know what happens next, who has the file now, and when.
  • Personal: feel that someone is really looking at the file, and that it is moving.
  • Social: have one person they can count on (Marc for Rafael and Pilar; a named owner for Laura's own clients).
  • All three get no value from the official status or codes (P3), and all three trust a person over a company (P2).

What changes from one end of the line to the other

  • Hard end (Rafael): the wait is long and the answer is always the same. Forgotten photos (P4) turn doubt about one step into doubt about the whole job. He checks in on a fixed weekday and keeps paper.
  • Regular (Pilar): the wait is short but unexplained. Her worry came only from not knowing the file was normal (P1), and her friction is calls at the wrong time.
  • Easy end (Laura): she has a known next step and an owner for each file, regular news every two weeks, and a checklist against missing papers. Her calm comes from knowing, not from speed (P1, P5).
  • Across the line, the distance is not in the length of the wait but in how much the person knows about it.

Which persona to design for first

Design first for Rafael. He is the hard end, so what works for him will also cover Pilar, whose worry is the same one in a milder form. His pains hold all the patterns (P1, P2, P3, P4), and he is the client most likely to lose trust in Voltia. Laura shows what good looks like, so she works as a reference for the team and not as the first user.
