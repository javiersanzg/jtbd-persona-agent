What this is

A persona is a fictional user built from real research. The challenge: clients of a small electrical installer in Valencia wait months for their electricity file and do not know what is happening. The agent writes three personas: the worst wait (hard end), the easiest (easy end) and a normal one (regular).

The patterns it found

Written by the agent. In italics, the insight: why it happens.

  1. P1

    The wait has no known length

    The same kind of file takes two weeks or two years, so most clients cannot tell if their wait is normal.

    Job to be done

    When my file is open, I want to know how long it will take, so I can plan my life around it.

    What people said

    «Hay expedientes que duran dos años o expedientes que duran dos semanas. Depende de lo que haya que hacer» (T). «En 2023 i-DE activó el 59% de los expedientes en menos de cinco días; y el 93% en menos de 20 días» (E).

  2. P2

    The file gets stuck between parties

    Each party points to another, and nobody owns the answer.

    Job to be done

    When my file stops moving, I want to know who holds it and why, so I know who to ask.

    What people said

    «La comercializadora me dice que la distribuidora rechaza la solicitud y la distribuidora que la comercializadora» (F, pantaleon). «Muchas veces los propios errores de las distribuidoras lo que hacen es empantanar la tramitación» (E).

  3. P3

    Paperwork and small gaps cause delays

    Missing signatures, photos and rejected documents add rounds, and Voltia goes back to a site 2 or 3 times per month.

    Job to be done

    When something is asked of me, I want to know exactly what and by when, so I do it right the first time.

    What people said

    «A veces el cliente se olvida de que tiene que firmar unos papeles y tienes que ir detrás de él, a veces a los compañeros se les olvida hacer una fotografía» (T). «Esta vez, que es la cuarta que los mandan, vuelven a estar rechazados» (F, Tevi62).

  4. P4

    Trust is built by honesty and by results

    Clients come by recommendation, and they judge the installer on honesty and on promises kept.

    Job to be done

    When I choose and keep an installer, I want honest words and agreed time and budget kept, so I can rely on them.

    What people said

    «Se intenta ser muy transparente en ese sentido» (T). «Generalmente por contactos y recomendaciones» (T). «Consiguieron ejecutarlo con el tiempo y presupuesto pactado» (R).

  5. P5

    Not knowing costs time and peace of mind

    Silence turns into repeated calls, lost hours and helplessness, for clients and for Voltia.

    Job to be done

    When I hear nothing, I want to stop chasing and feel in control, so I can get on with my day.

    What people said

    «Yo llamo por teléfono día sí y día no para preguntar siempre me dicen lo mismo» (F, Julio21). «Este trabajo me está llevando 5 horas diarias» (F, pantaleon). «impotencia» (F, Tevi62). «Están constantemente comunicándose con nosotros» (T).

Hard end · A file that is very slow or blocked

Lucia, 41

“Está todo perfectamente realizado y no puedo tener luz por este trámite”

PASS441 wordschecked by check_persona.py

Profile

  • Lives in a new house in a town near Valencia*.
  • Client of Voltia, who did the installation.
  • Her new supply file with i-DE is stuck.

Background

Lucia built the house with her family*. Voltia finished the electrical work and it is "perfectly done". The file with the distributor has been open for many months*. Voltia told her at the start that it might take long*, and she believed it was a matter of weeks*.

Goals

  • Get electricity in the house.
  • Understand why the file does not move.
  • Stop spending her days on this.

Pains and frustrations

  • The retailer says the distributor rejects the request, and the distributor says the retailer does (P2).
  • Documents come back rejected, even after she sends them again (P3).
  • She calls every other day and hears the same answer (P5).
  • Nobody tells her whether two months or two years is normal (P1).
  • She lives with no power and pays rent elsewhere*.

Jobs to be done

  • Functional: know who holds my file and what is missing, so I can act.
  • Personal: stop feeling helpless and stop chasing people.
  • Social: show my family that the delay is not my fault*.

A day in their life during the wait

Morning: she phones the retailer*. Midday: she phones the distributor* and then Voltia. She checks her email for any reply*. Afternoon: she looks for the document they asked for and sends it again*. Evening: she tells her partner "no news" and reads forums about other people with the same problem*. About five hours go to this on bad days (P5).

How they look for news today

  • Phone calls, first to the retailer, then to the distributor, then to Voltia (P2).
  • Voltia is the only party that picks up and is honest with her* (P4).
  • Online forums, to see if her wait is normal (P1).
  • She gets no news on her own; she has to ask.

What makes them trust or doubt an installer

  • Trust: Voltia tells her the truth, even when the news is bad (P4).
  • Trust: Voltia did the work well and on time* (P4).
  • Doubt: if Voltia cannot say where the file is, she starts to think Voltia has lost control of it (P2).
  • Doubt: each new call that ends with "we do not know" (P5).

Based on

  • Profile and Background: P1 (wait with no known length)
  • Goals and Jobs to be done: P2 (file stuck between parties)
  • Pains: P3 (rejected documents), P5 (cost of not knowing)
  • Trust and doubt: P4 (honesty and results)

*Not in the research: invented to make her real.

Easy end · Follows many files with ease

Daniel, 52

“Solo quiero saber cuál espera por mí*”

PASS451 wordschecked by check_persona.py

Profile

  • Manages six small commercial premises in Valencia*.
  • Has several electrical files open with Voltia at the same time*.
  • Has done this many times and knows how it works.

Background

Daniel came to Voltia through a recommendation (P4). He has seen files take two weeks and files take much longer, and he knows that the length depends on what has to be done (P1). He keeps a short list of his files in a notebook*. Most of them close without trouble, as the i-DE CEO says: 93% of self-consumption files are active in under 20 days (P1).

Goals

  • Keep all his premises running and legal.
  • Know which files need something from him this week.
  • Spend almost no time on the files that are moving.

Pains and frustrations

  • Having to repeat a signature or a document he already gave (P3).
  • Finding out late that a file was rejected (P2).
  • The odd file that stops without a reason and breaks his routine (P5).
  • Not knowing if one slow file is normal or a sign of a problem (P1).

Jobs to be done

  • Functional: see at a glance which of my files wait for me and which wait for others.
  • Personal: stay calm and stay in control without chasing anyone.
  • Social: show the owners of the premises that everything is on track*.

A day in their life during the wait

Daniel does not spend a day on the files. Once a week* he calls Voltia and asks Marc* for the state of each one. He answers fast if Voltia asks for a paper, because he knows a delay on his side stops everything (P3). Most days he forgets the files exist. When a file goes quiet for weeks, he calls once more, and then he waits*.

How they look for news today

  • He asks Voltia by phone or WhatsApp*, not the distributor (P2).
  • He trusts the answer, because Voltia is honest about delays (P4).
  • He checks the dates in his notebook*, to see if a file is late against his own experience (P1).

What makes them trust or doubt an installer

  • Trust: clear words, with no promises that cannot be kept (P4).
  • Trust: the agreed time and budget are kept (P4).
  • Doubt: a team that forgets a photo or a paper and returns to the site (P3).
  • Doubt: silence for weeks, even when nothing is wrong (P5).

Based on

  • Profile and Background: P1 (length varies) and P4 (recommendation)
  • Goals and Jobs to be done: P2 (who holds the file)
  • Pains: P3 (paperwork), P5 (cost of not knowing)
  • Trust and doubt: P4 (honesty and results)

*Not in the research: invented to make him real.

Regular · A normal file

Carmen, 38

“¿Esto es normal, o debería preguntar?*”

PASS401 wordschecked by check_persona.py

Profile

  • Teacher living in a flat in Valencia*.
  • Client of Voltia for a solar self-consumption installation*.
  • Her file with i-DE is open and moving at a normal pace.

Background

Carmen chose Voltia after a friend recommended them (P4). Voltia told her the file may take long and was transparent about it (P1). The installation is finished. She had to sign some papers, and she signed them a week late* (P3). She does not know much about how the file works.

Goals

  • Start using her solar panels as soon as the file allows it.
  • Know roughly when that will be.
  • Not give the file more time than needed.

Pains and frustrations

  • She cannot tell if three weeks is fast or slow (P1).
  • She does not know what is waiting on her and what is waiting on Iberdrola (P2).
  • A request for one more paper arrives with no warning (P3).
  • She feels she is bothering Voltia when she asks, but she asks anyway (P5).

Jobs to be done

  • Functional: know what step my file is on and what comes next.
  • Personal: feel sure that nothing is lost or forgotten.
  • Social: tell my partner and neighbours a date with some confidence*.

A day in their life during the wait

She works as normal. Every few days she remembers the file and wonders if she should ask*. On Friday* she sends Voltia a message: «any news?»* She gets an honest answer, often "still waiting" (P4). She feels better for a day, and then the cycle starts again (P5).

How they look for news today

  • She writes or calls Voltia, the only contact she has (P5).
  • She does not call the distributor, because she believes it is Voltia's job* (P2).
  • She asks friends who went through the same file*.

What makes them trust or doubt an installer

  • Trust: the recommendation and a good first meeting (P4).
  • Trust: Voltia does what it said, at the price it said (P4).
  • Doubt: a vague answer, or no answer for a week (P5).
  • Doubt: Voltia coming back for a photo it forgot (P3).

Based on

  • Profile and Background: P4 (recommendation) and P1 (wait length)
  • Goals and Jobs to be done: P2 (who holds the file)
  • Pains: P3 (paperwork), P5 (cost of not knowing)
  • Trust and doubt: P4 (honesty and results)

*Not in the research: invented to make her real.

No persona needed a failed fix. Daniel and Carmen are built mostly from invented detail (marked with *), because the research has no interview with a client of this kind. Lucia rests on forum posts, not on a Voltia client.

What the three share

  • Know where the file is and what comes next (P1, P2).
  • Know what is asked of them and do it right the first time (P3).
  • Be told the truth by the installer, with promises kept (P4).
  • Feel in control without having to chase anyone (P5).
  • All three go to Voltia first, not to the distributor.

What changes from one end of the line to the other

  • Time: Lucia's wait has no end she can see (P1). Daniel knows files can take two weeks or two years. Carmen cannot tell if her wait is normal.
  • Who holds the file: for Lucia the file is stuck between retailer and distributor (P2). Daniel and Carmen do not face this, but Carmen does not know what waits on her.
  • Cost of not knowing: Lucia loses hours and peace (P5). Daniel loses almost nothing. Carmen loses a little each week.
  • Trust: Lucia keeps trusting Voltia only because Voltia is honest (P4). Daniel trusts from experience. Carmen trusts from the recommendation.
  • Paperwork: rejected documents cost Lucia months (P3). For Daniel and Carmen one forgotten signature costs days.

Who to design for first

The team should design for Lucia first. Her pains are the strongest in the research and they hit all five patterns, and her need to know where the file is and who holds it is also what Daniel and Carmen need in a smaller way. Voltia hears the same pain from its own clients, who "are constantly contacting us" (Toni), so this end carries the cost for both clients and staff. Carmen is the second choice, because her case is the most common and the easiest to test. One limit: Lucia comes from outside forums, so the team should check her with a real Voltia client.
