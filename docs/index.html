How it works

  1. Goal

    Three personas, one per point of the research line, each passing the checker, plus a report that compares them. Only the research as input: no web, no invented data, no solutions.

  2. Brain

    Claude, running headless in Claude Code. It chooses its next step on its own.

  3. Tools

    Read the research, write files, and run check_persona.py: sections in order, all five patterns used, every section traced to a pattern, invented details flagged, no solutions, one page.

  4. Memory

    A step log it writes as it works. Think, act, observe, reflect: every decision is on the record below.

Hard end · A file that is very slow or blocked

Raul*, the client whose file is stuck

“”

PASS399 wordschecked by check_persona.py

> "Everything is done perfectly and I cannot have electricity because of this paperwork."

Profile

Homeowner waiting for a new supply or a power increase*. Age 45*. He writes in public forums at night.

Background

His installer finished the work. The file with i-DE has been open for months, past the legal 30 working days. He has heard of people who wait 6 months. Retailer and distributor each say the other one is the problem. He has called again and again.

Goals

  • Have light and power at home again.
  • Understand why the file is stopped.
  • Stop spending hours on it.

Pains and frustrations

  • Every call gets "the same" answer and nothing moves.
  • A rejected paper goes back, and is rejected a fourth time.
  • He is cold at home, with a few light bulbs.
  • Hours of daily work: "5 hours a day".
  • His last words are "desperate" and "helplessness".

Jobs to be done

  • Functional: know where the file is, who has it, and what comes next, without coordinating it himself.
  • Personal: get his normal life back and stop feeling powerless.
  • Social: not look foolish to the person who recommended the installer*.

A day in their life during the wait

He phones every other day. At 2 a.m. he writes a post. In the afternoon he writes another. He sends the same papers again. He checks the file between calls*. At night he asks strangers about his rights: "Can I report them?"

How they look for news today

He phones the retailer, then the distributor, then the installer*. He reads forums to compare his wait with others. He gets a different answer from each, so he starts again.

What makes them trust or doubt an installer

He doubts when the work is done and still nothing works. He wants a named person who answers for his case*. He trusts less when the installer only repeats what the distributor says*.

Based on

  • Profile and Background: P2 (nobody owns the file), P5 (finished work, no use).
  • Goals: P1 (know where the file is), P5.
  • Pains and frustrations: P1 (repeated calls), P3 (papers rejected again), P5 (helplessness).
  • Jobs to be done: P1 and P2 (functional), P5 (personal), P4 (social).
  • A day in their life: P1, P2 (he manages the file), P3.
  • How they look for news: P1, P2.
  • Trust or doubt: P4 (a named person), P5.

Easy end · Follows many files with ease

Nuria*, the client who follows many files with ease

“”

PASS434 wordschecked by check_persona.py

> "They did it in the agreed time and budget, with a good finish."

Profile

Runs projects in Valencia and hires installers often. Judges a job by time and budget, the two things she must answer for. Age and family are not in the research*.

Background

She gave Voltia a hard project with very little time. It was finished on the agreed time and budget. She plans to hire Voltia again. She thanked one person by name and wrote a public review. She has several jobs open at once*. Many open files are normal for her: another manager has 15 installations at once, though his were stuck for 15 months.

Goals

  • Keep every project on time and on budget.
  • Know that one person answers for each job.
  • Hire the same installer again without a new search.

Pains and frustrations

  • A file that is delayed puts her own deadline at risk.
  • A missing signature or photo can send a job back*.
  • She has no time to chase papers on many files*.
  • Delays she cannot see are the main risk for her*.

Jobs to be done

  • Functional: know where each file is and what comes next, with little effort (P1).
  • Personal: feel that someone she knows is responsible for each case (P4).
  • Social: look good before the people she recommends an installer to (P4).

A day in their life during the wait

She checks on several jobs between meetings*. She does not phone every day. She asks the person she knows, Marc, when something is unclear*. Without news, her wait is still calm, not desperate. She does not describe herself as helpless, as the clients with stuck files do (P5).

How they look for news today

She goes to a person, not to a company line. The research shows her thanking Marc by name, and Toni says Marc deals most with clients. She uses the same contact each time*.

What makes them trust or doubt an installer

She trusts a promise kept: time, budget, finish. She trusts a named person. She came through contacts and recommendations, like most clients. She would doubt if a file started to stall and no one told her (P2, P3).

Based on

  • Profile and Background: P4 (trust in a person), P1 (many files, calm).
  • Goals: P4, P2 (someone answers for the file).
  • Pains and frustrations: P3 (errors found late), P1.
  • Jobs to be done: P1 (functional), P4 (personal and social).
  • A day in their life: P1, P5 (what she avoids).
  • How they look for news: P4, P1.
  • Trust or doubt: P4, P2, P3.

Regular · A normal file

Marta*, the client with a normal file

“”

PASS476 wordschecked by check_persona.py

> "They are constantly contacting us because these processes take a long time." (Toni, about clients like her)

Profile

Homeowner or small business owner* with one electrical file open through Voltia. She came by a recommendation. No forum posts from this point of the line exist in the research, so most of her is built from Toni and i-DE's figures.

Background

The work is going normally. Voltia told her at the start that the file may take long. Files last from two weeks to two years, and nobody can tell her where hers will fall. i-DE says most files are quick: 93% in under 20 days (self-consumption files only), and 2% take more than 60 days. Age and job are not in the research*.

Goals

  • Have the file finished without surprises.
  • Know if her wait is normal.
  • Not have to learn how the process works*.

Pains and frustrations

  • She does not know if two weeks of silence is normal or the start of a problem.
  • She may be asked to sign papers late, because the client forgets and Voltia has to chase.
  • A photo may be missing and the team goes back to the site (four or five times a month).
  • A rejection could turn a normal file into a long one.

Jobs to be done

  • Functional: know where my file is, what is missing and what comes next (P1).
  • Personal: feel that someone I know is responsible for my case (P4).
  • Social: not regret recommending Voltia to the person who asked me*.

A day in their life during the wait

She does not think about the file most days*. After some time without news she writes or calls Voltia to ask if it is normal. She hears that it may take long. She waits again. She knows the retailer and the distributor exist (P2), but she is not yet chasing them*.

How they look for news today

She contacts Voltia, likely the person she already knows, Marc*. She does not phone a company line, because she has not yet been sent from one party to the other. Each call is a small test of the same question.

What makes them trust or doubt an installer

She trusts the honesty at the start: "we try to be very transparent". She trusts a known person and a recommendation. She starts to doubt when the answers repeat and the days pass*. If papers are rejected again (P3), she may move toward the hard end (P5)*.

Based on

  • Profile and Background: P1 (the wait is long), P4 (recommendation).
  • Goals: P1, P3 (no surprises).
  • Pains and frustrations: P1, P3, P2.
  • Jobs to be done: P1 (functional), P4 (personal and social).
  • A day in their life: P1, P2.
  • How they look for news: P1, P4.
  • Trust or doubt: P4, P3, P5.

What the agent concluded

Challenge: clients with an Iberdrola file or an electrical certificate in progress do not know what is happening with it. Patterns P1 to P5 are in patterns.md.

Where the research is thin

  • Persona 1 is the best supported: four real forum users. These people are not Voltia clients.
  • Persona 2 rests on one review (N. M.) and one expert (Herrera, whose files were stuck, not easy). Her "many files" is partly inferred*.
  • Persona 3 has no direct voice in the research. It comes from Toni's interview and i-DE's figures, which cover self-consumption files only. It carries the most asterisks.

Which persona to design for first

Design first for Marta (persona 3). She is the client Toni describes when he says clients are "constantly" in contact, and she is the point where a normal file can turn into a bad one (P1, then P3 and P2). Raul is the clearest case, but he is the rarest one in i-DE's figures (2% of files over 60 days), so he works best as a test of the work and not as its target. Because Marta's evidence is the thinnest, the team should confirm her with real Voltia clients before relying on her.
