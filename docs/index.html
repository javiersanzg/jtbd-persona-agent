What this is

A persona is a fictional user built from real research. The challenge: clients of a small electrical installer in Valencia wait months for their electricity file and do not know what is happening. The agent writes three personas: the worst wait (hard end), the easiest (easy end) and a normal one (regular).

  2. 2

    check_persona.py checks each persona. FAIL means fix and retry, up to 3 times.

The patterns it found

Written by the agent. In italics, the insight: why it happens.

  1. P1

    Waiting without news

    the client calls because there is no other source of news, and each call returns the same answer.

    Job to be done

    know where my file is and what happens next, without having to ask.

    What people said

    «Yo llamo por teléfono día sí y día no para preguntar siempre me dicen lo mismo y no me dan solución alguna» (Julio21). «Están constantemente comunicándose con nosotros porque son procesos que se alargan mucho» (Toni).

  2. P2

    Nobody owns the problem

    blame passes between retailer, distributor and installer, so the client cannot tell who to ask.

    Job to be done

    find out who is responsible for my file and who can move it.

    What people said

    «La comercializadora me dice que la distribuidora rechaza la solicitud y la distribuidora que la comercializadora» (pantaleon). «Muchas veces los propios errores de las distribuidoras lo que hacen es empantanar la tramitación» (Joan Herrera).

  3. P3

    Duration nobody can predict

    the same type of file can take days or years, so a client cannot tell if their wait is normal.

    Job to be done

    plan my life (light, heating, solar) around a date I can trust.

    What people said

    «Hay expedientes que duran dos años o expedientes que duran dos semanas. Depende de lo que haya que hacer» (Toni). «En 2023 i-DE activó el 59% de los expedientes en menos de cinco días; y el 93% en menos de 20 días» (CEO of i-DE).

  4. P4

    Paperwork loops

    files stall on small missing or rejected items, from the client and from the installer.

    Job to be done

    get my documents accepted the first time and know what is missing.

    What people said

    «Esta vez, que es la cuarta que los mandan, vuelven a estar rechazados» (Tevi62). «A veces el cliente se olvida de que tiene que firmar unos papeles y tienes que ir detrás de él, a veces a los compañeros se les olvida hacer una fotografía» (Toni).

  5. P5

    Trust comes from honesty and people

    clients choose Voltia by recommendation and keep trusting it when it is clear about delays.

    Job to be done

    feel that my installer is straight with me, even when the news is bad.

    What people said

    «Se intenta ser muy transparente en ese sentido» (Toni). «Generalmente por contactos y recomendaciones» (Toni). «Consiguieron ejecutarlo con el tiempo y presupuesto pactado» (N. M.).

Hard end · A file that is very slow or blocked

Carmen

“Llevo así desde el día 3 de octubre de 2023, pasando frío y con la única luz de unas bombillas, no funciona nada”

PASS347 wordschecked by check_persona.py

Profile

Carmen*, 54*, lives in a house near Valencia*. She waits for a power increase. Her case is the extreme one seen in the forum posts.

Background

The installer finished the work. The file stayed between the retailer and the distributor, and months passed. She worked in a school office*.

Goals

  • Have working electricity again (P3).
  • Know who is responsible for her file (P2).
  • Stop spending hours on the phone (P1).

Pains and frustrations

  • «La comercializadora me dice que la distribuidora rechaza la solicitud y la distribuidora que la comercializadora» (P2).
  • Every call gets the same answer (P1).
  • Her papers come back rejected, again (P4).
  • She does not know if her wait is normal (P3).

Jobs to be done

  • Functional: find out the exact state of her file and what is missing (P1, P4).
  • Personal: stop feeling helpless and abandoned (P2).
  • Social: not look foolish to her family* for choosing this work (P5).

A day in their life during the wait

Morning*: cold house, a few light bulbs. She phones the retailer, then the distributor* (P1). At lunch she writes to Voltia*. In the evening she checks her email* and finds nothing. She keeps a folder with every paper sent* because she fears another rejection (P4).

How they look for news today

She calls every other day (P1). She calls Voltia first because it is the one who answers*. At night she reads forums* to see if others wait as long (P3).

What makes them trust or doubt an installer

She doubts anyone who only says "it depends". She trusts the one who says early that the file may take long (P5). She loses trust when each party points at another (P2).

Easy end · Follows many files with ease

Rafa

“Grandes profesionales. [...] Consiguieron ejecutarlo con el tiempo y presupuesto pactado”

PASS373 wordschecked by check_persona.py

Profile

Rafa*, 46*, manages the electrical work of many small buildings in Valencia*. He follows several files at once and they usually go well. The research has few people like him: only the good Google review and the i-DE figures point to this end.

Background

He found Voltia through a contact*, as most clients do (P5). Most of his files fall in the fast group: 59% of solar files were activated in under five days, 93% in under 20 days (P3).

Goals

  • Keep every job inside the agreed time and budget (P5).
  • Know in a minute which of his files is waiting for what (P1).
  • Never be the reason a file stops (P4).

Pains and frustrations

  • The few slow files hurt more because he has many (P3).
  • A missing signature or photo can stop one file (P4).
  • He does not always know if the delay is Voltia, the retailer or the distributor (P2).

Jobs to be done

  • Functional: follow many files and spot the one that has stopped (P1, P3).
  • Personal: feel in control, without chasing anybody (P2).
  • Social: look organised to the owners he works for* (P5).

A day in their life during the wait

He opens a list he keeps himself* each morning (P1). He signs what is pending the same day* (P4). He calls Voltia only for the file that looks late* (P3). He accepts "it may take long" if he is told early (P5).

How they look for news today

He asks Marc or Toni directly* and gets an honest answer (P5). He rarely phones the distributor (P2). His own list is the main source* (P1).

What makes them trust or doubt an installer

He trusts people who finish on time and budget and who are transparent (P5). He doubts one who is silent while a file is stuck (P1). A paper that has to be sent twice makes him doubt (P4).

Regular · A normal file

Elena

“Está todo perfectamente realizado y no puedo tener luz por este trámite”

PASS366 wordschecked by check_persona.py

Profile

Elena*, 38*, has a new home in Valencia* with a new supply. Her file is normal: not days, not years. The quote is from a forum user, Julio21, whose case is similar.

Background

A friend recommended Voltia (P5). The installation is done. Now the file is with the distributor, and nobody tells her how long it will take (P3).

Goals

  • Get the light on by a date she can plan around (P3).
  • Know what is happening, without calling (P1).
  • Not make a mistake with her papers (P4).

Pains and frustrations

  • She phones every few days and hears the same answer (P1).
  • She is not sure if she must sign or send something (P4).
  • She does not know who to ask: Voltia, retailer or distributor (P2).
  • She cannot tell if the wait is normal (P3).

Jobs to be done

  • Functional: know the state of her file and the next step (P1, P4).
  • Personal: feel calm while the house cannot be used* (P3).
  • Social: answer her partner* when they ask "when?" (P5).

A day in their life during the wait

She works all day* and writes to Voltia at lunch* (P1). In the evening she looks for the last email* and finds nothing. She wonders if she forgot a signature* (P4). At the weekend she asks a friend* how long theirs took (P3).

How they look for news today

She contacts Voltia by phone and message* (P1). Toni tells her the file may take long and is clear about it (P5). She does not know the distributor's channels* (P2).

What makes them trust or doubt an installer

She trusts Voltia because of the recommendation (P5). She trusts more when she is told the truth about delays (P5). She doubts when she gets no answer for days (P1) or when she hears a different story from each party (P2).

What the three share (common jobs to be done)

  • Know where the file is and what comes next (P1).
  • Know who is responsible and who can move it (P2).
  • Be sure the papers are complete and accepted (P4).
  • Feel that the installer is straight about delays (P5).
  • Understand if the wait is normal (P3).

What changes from one end of the line to the other

  • Hard end: the wait is long and the blame passes between parties (P2, P3). The persona feels helpless and phones often (P1). Rejected papers repeat (P4).
  • Regular: the wait is normal but unclear. She does not know how long it will take or who to ask (P1, P3).
  • Easy end: many files, most of them fast. The few slow ones stand out (P3). He trusts because Voltia is on time and honest (P5).

The hard end lives in the same pains as the other two, but much stronger. The trust from P5 is what stays across the whole line.

Which persona to design for first

Design first for Elena, the regular client. The research shows that most files are normal and that clients call Voltia constantly because they do not know what is happening (P1). Her needs sit in the middle: if the team understands them, it covers most of the common jobs. Carmen is the strongest pain, but her case is extreme and comes only from forum posts, not from Voltia clients.

Limits of the research

  • Only one Voltia interview (Toni), one Google review and public posts. Most personal details are invented and marked with an asterisk.
  • The easy end has the thinnest evidence: no source describes a user who follows many files with ease. Rafa is built from the good review, Toni's answers and the i-DE figures. Treat him as a hypothesis to check in new interviews.

  4. Results in output/, steps in memory/log.md. This page is rebuilt each run.
