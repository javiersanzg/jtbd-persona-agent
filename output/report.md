## What the three share (common jobs to be done)
- Know where the file is and what comes next (P1).
- Know who is responsible and who can move it (P2).
- Be sure the papers are complete and accepted (P4).
- Feel that the installer is straight about delays (P5).
- Understand if the wait is normal (P3).

## What changes from one end of the line to the other
- **Hard end:** the wait is long and the blame passes between parties (P2, P3). The persona feels helpless and phones often (P1). Rejected papers repeat (P4).
- **Regular:** the wait is normal but unclear. She does not know how long it will take or who to ask (P1, P3).
- **Easy end:** many files, most of them fast. The few slow ones stand out (P3). He trusts because Voltia is on time and honest (P5).

The hard end lives in the same pains as the other two, but much stronger. The trust from P5 is what stays across the whole line.

## Which persona to design for first
Design first for Elena, the regular client. The research shows that most files are normal and that clients call Voltia constantly because they do not know what is happening (P1). Her needs sit in the middle: if the team understands them, it covers most of the common jobs. Carmen is the strongest pain, but her case is extreme and comes only from forum posts, not from Voltia clients.

## Limits of the research
- Only one Voltia interview (Toni), one Google review and public posts. Most personal details are invented and marked with an asterisk.
- The easy end has the thinnest evidence: no source describes a user who follows many files with ease. Rafa is built from the good review, Toni's answers and the i-DE figures. Treat him as a hypothesis to check in new interviews.
