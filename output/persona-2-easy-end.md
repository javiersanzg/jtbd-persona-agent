# Nuria* - "They did it in the agreed time and budget, with a good finish."

## Profile
Runs projects in Valencia and follows many jobs and files at once, with a team around her*. She judges a job by time and budget, the two things she must answer for. Her age is not in the research*.

## Background
She gave Voltia a project that was hard to do, with very little time. The job went well and she plans to hire Voltia again. She came, or would come, through contacts and recommendations. Her files are many, so she counts them, like the city official with 15 installations.

## Goals
- Keep the promise she made to the people she answers to.
- Know that each file is moving without checking all of them herself.
- Work again with the same installer on the next projects.

## Pains and frustrations
- Her files still depend on a process she does not control (P2).
- A rejection can come weeks after its cause, like an error in a document or one made by the distributor (P3).
- She does not know if a file will take "two weeks or two years" (P1).
- When one file is blocked, the finished work cannot be used (P5)*. She has less time for it than a single client*.

## Jobs to be done
- **Functional:** know where each file is and what comes next (P1); have each one accepted the first time (P3).
- **Personal:** feel safe because she knows one person by name (P4).
- **Social:** keep her word to the people she answers to, with the time and budget agreed (P4).

## A day in their life during the wait
She goes through her jobs in the morning*. For most she needs nothing, because a person she trusts has told her the file may take long, in a transparent way (P4). She calls only for the one that is out of line*. In the evening she tells her own people what to expect* (P1).

## How they look for news today
She asks the person she knows, Marc, not the company or the distributor (P4). She does not post in forums*. Clients "constantly contact" Voltia, so she may be one of many callers (P1).

## What makes them trust or doubt an installer
She trusts the kept promise: agreed time, agreed budget, good finish, and a person to thank by name (P4). She would doubt after a promise broken by someone she trusted, or a delay that began in the job, such as a missing signature or photo (P3). If the work is done but power is not live, trust falls (P5)*.
