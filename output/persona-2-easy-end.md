# Daniel, 52* — «Solo quiero saber cuál espera por mí»*

## Profile
- Manages six small commercial premises in Valencia*.
- Has several electrical files open with Voltia at the same time*.
- Has done this many times and knows how it works.

## Background
Daniel came to Voltia through a recommendation (P4). He has seen files take two weeks and files take much longer, and he knows that the length depends on what has to be done (P1). He keeps a short list of his files in a notebook*. Most of them close without trouble, as the i-DE CEO says: 93% of self-consumption files are active in under 20 days (P1).

## Goals
- Keep all his premises running and legal.
- Know which files need something from him this week.
- Spend almost no time on the files that are moving.

## Pains and frustrations
- Having to repeat a signature or a document he already gave (P3).
- Finding out late that a file was rejected (P2).
- The odd file that stops without a reason and breaks his routine (P5).
- Not knowing if one slow file is normal or a sign of a problem (P1).

## Jobs to be done
- Functional: see at a glance which of my files wait for me and which wait for others.
- Personal: stay calm and stay in control without chasing anyone.
- Social: show the owners of the premises that everything is on track*.

## A day in their life during the wait
Daniel does not spend a day on the files. Once a week* he calls Voltia and asks Marc* for the state of each one. He answers fast if Voltia asks for a paper, because he knows a delay on his side stops everything (P3). Most days he forgets the files exist. When a file goes quiet for weeks, he calls once more, and then he waits*.

## How they look for news today
- He asks Voltia by phone or WhatsApp*, not the distributor (P2).
- He trusts the answer, because Voltia is honest about delays (P4).
- He checks the dates in his notebook*, to see if a file is late against his own experience (P1).

## What makes them trust or doubt an installer
- Trust: clear words, with no promises that cannot be kept (P4).
- Trust: the agreed time and budget are kept (P4).
- Doubt: a team that forgets a photo or a paper and returns to the site (P3).
- Doubt: silence for weeks, even when nothing is wrong (P5).

## Based on
- Profile and Background: P1 (length varies) and P4 (recommendation)
- Goals and Jobs to be done: P2 (who holds the file)
- Pains: P3 (paperwork), P5 (cost of not knowing)
- Trust and doubt: P4 (honesty and results)

*Not in the research: invented to make him real.
